Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how DSCLOUD collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

File Metadata: When you upload files or text, we collect information about the files (name, size, type, upload date).

Technical Data: We collect IP addresses for rate limiting, security, and service optimization purposes.

Authentication Data: If you use Google OAuth or email verification, we collect the data necessary for authentication.

Usage Statistics: We track views, downloads, and basic analytics to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Service Delivery: To store, retrieve, and deliver your files and content.

Security: To protect against abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access through rate limiting and IP tracking.

Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our platform.

Communication: To send service-related notifications and security alerts if necessary.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your files are stored on secure cloud servers (Vercel Blob Storage). We maintain references in our MongoDB database. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.

All data transmissions are encrypted using HTTPS. However, we recommend not uploading highly sensitive or personally identifiable information.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage only to remember your upload history on your device. This data is stored locally and never transmitted to our servers.

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools that track your behavior across the web.

5. File Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your files. Files can be deleted at any time using the unique delete key provided during upload.

We do not automatically delete files after a certain period unless you set an expiration date on your content.

When you delete a file, it is permanently removed from our servers and cannot be recovered.

6. Third-Party Services

Google OAuth: We use Google's authentication service. Google's privacy policy applies to data collected during authentication.

Email Service: We use email services for verification and notifications. Your email address is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your files at any time
  • Download or delete your files
  • Request deletion of your account data
  • Understand how your data is used

8. Data Retention

We retain file metadata as long as the file exists in our system. Access logs and IP data are retained for security purposes and are automatically purged after 90 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through email: support.dscloud@gmail.com
